XUONG VIDEO ANIMATION - BAN CHAY RIENG (KHONG CAN CLAUDE, KHONG CAN MANG)

MAY TINH (Windows)
1. Giai nen file zip ra mot thu muc (vi du Documents\XuongVideo).
2. Bam dup file CHAY_XUONG_VIDEO.bat. Trinh duyet tu mo trang Xuong video.
   (Lan dau Windows co the hoi "Windows protected your PC": bam "More info" > "Run anyway".)
3. Lan dau bam "Lam video", may doc giong mat 1-2 phut. Tu lan sau chay ca khi tat mang.
4. Bam "Tai video ve may": video vao thu muc Downloads.
5. Giu cua so den mo trong luc lam. Lam xong thi dong cua so den va trang web.

Khong mo truc tiep file index.html (trang se bao loi), luon mo bang CHAY_XUONG_VIDEO.bat.
Nen dung Chrome hoac Edge.

DIEN THOAI
Can dua thu muc nay len mot trang web mien phi (lam 1 lan), roi mo tren dien thoai va "Them vao Man hinh chinh".
Sau lan mo dau (tai khoang 110 MB), dien thoai chay duoc khi tat mang.
